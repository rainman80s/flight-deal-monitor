            About Fare Radar

            Fare Radar is a small, independent site that watches round-trip fares out of Seattle (SEA) and Portland (PDX) around the clock and lists the cheap ones. It started as a tool for a couple of friends who travel a lot. Anyone can browse the deals for free; members get alerts, their own rules and route search.

            No ads, no affiliate links, no commissions. We don't earn anything when you book a flight.

            Questions

            Where do the fares come from?

            From Google Flights results (fetched through SerpApi) and from Ignav's fare search. We check about once an hour and double-check a fare before we alert anyone about it.

            Why did the price change when I clicked through?

            Airfares change constantly. A fare here is the price we saw when we found it, and cheap seats can sell out in minutes. Always check the final price before you pay.

            How do you decide what counts as a deal?

            Each column has its own rules for how far out the trip leaves, how long it is and how cheap it has to be. For US trips we also learn each route's usual price and flag fares well below it. The rules show under each column header, and members can set their own on the Logic page.

            Can I book on this site?

            You book with the airline or through Google Flights: open any deal and use the buttons there. (Booking right here is limited to the site's owners for now.)

            What do members get?

            For $8 a month: deal alerts by email, judged by your own rules; your own logic (how far out, how long, how cheap, and "well below the usual price" alerts), which changes only your view; and route search across a whole month of dates. Anyone can browse the deals for free.

            How do I cancel?

            On your Account page, choose Manage billing, then cancel. You keep your membership until the end of the month you've paid for. You can also delete your account, which cancels right away.

            How do I sign in?

            With your email and password, or with Google or Apple where offered. Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen and we'll email a link that works once, for 30 minutes.

            Which airports do you cover?

            Departures from Seattle-Tacoma (SEA) and Portland (PDX) for now, to about 50 cities in the US and abroad. Prices are round trip, per person, economy, in US dollars.

            Something's wrong or missing.

            Send us a note. We read everything.

            Privacy policy

            Effective November 11, 2026

            The short version: we collect as little as we can. No analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your personal information.

            What we collect

            • Your account. Your email address, the name you give (if any), your password (only as a one-way hash), whether you signed in with Google or Apple, and when you created the account and last signed in.
            • Your settings. Your home airports, whether alerts are on, your own deal rules, and which deals we've emailed you about, so we never send the same one twice.
            • Membership. Your Stripe customer number and your membership status and renewal date. Stripe holds the payment details.
            • Contact form. Your name if you give it, your email and your message. It's delivered to us by email and isn't stored on the site.
            • Owners' booking details. Only the site's owners can book. The traveler details they save for that (names, dates of birth, contact details, passport details) are stored encrypted and used only to book their flights.
            • Abuse protection. To limit sign-ups, sign-ins, searches and messages per visitor, we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, never the address itself. These records are deleted after 7 days.

            Route searches you run on the site are saved without anything that identifies you, and help us learn each route's usual price.

            What we don't do

            • No analytics or tracking scripts, and no advertising.
            • No selling or sharing of personal information, as "sell" and "share" are defined by California law (CCPA/CPRA). There's nothing to opt out of.
            • No marketing email. You only get the alerts you asked for.

            Cookies

            The site sets a sign-in cookie, __Host-fr_session, only when you sign in. It keeps you signed in for up to 30 days (or until 14 days of not visiting) and is used for nothing else. When you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple", a second cookie lasts 10 minutes to check the sign-in comes back to the same browser. Visitors who don't sign in get no cookies, which is why there's no cookie banner.

            Services that handle data for us

            • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database. Like any web host, it sees visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages.
            • Stripe handles membership payments. It receives your email and the card details you enter on Stripe's own checkout page; we never see your card number.
            • Google or Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them. They tell us your email address and name, and we never get your password for those services.
            • Resend sends our emails.
            • Duffel and the airline receive traveler details when an owner books a flight.
            • SerpApi and Ignav answer our fare searches. They never receive any of your personal information.

            Links to Google Flights and airline sites are covered by those companies' own privacy policies.

            How we protect it

            • Your email address and name are encrypted in our database (AES-256). We find your account with a keyed, one-way fingerprint of your email, not the address itself.
            • Passwords are never stored. We keep only a salted, slow hash (PBKDF2 with 100,000 rounds plus a server-side secret), so even we can't see your password.
            • Sign-in sessions are random 256-bit values; the database holds only a fingerprint of each, and you can end them all from your Account page. Repeated wrong passwords lock the account for a while.
            • The whole site runs over HTTPS and loads no third-party scripts.

            How long we keep it

            Your account, rules and alert history stay until you delete your account on the Account page, which removes them right away and cancels your membership. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires. Sign-in sessions end after 30 days and password-reset links after 30 minutes. Owners' traveler details stay until they delete them.

            Your choices and rights

            You can see, download, correct or delete the information we hold about you, wherever you live. That includes rights under California, Washington and EU/UK law. Most of it you can do yourself on the Account page. For anything else, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can stop alerts any time with the link in every email.

            Children

            The site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

            Accessibility

            We want everyone to be able to find and use a cheap fare here, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, zoom or other assistive technology.

            What we aim for

            We aim for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

            • Every control works with a keyboard and shows a visible focus ring.
            • Text keeps strong contrast over the background photo, which is decorative only.
            • Forms have labels, and errors and results are announced to screen readers.
            • Animation is minimal and turns off when your device asks for reduced motion.
            • The layout works from small phones to large screens and at 200% zoom.

            Known limits

            • The month price calendar in "Check a route" is a dense grid. Screen readers read each day as a list item.
            • Fares link to Google Flights and airline sites, which we don't control.

            If something on the site doesn't work for you, tell us what you were trying to do and what you use (browser, screen reader, device). We'll reply within a few days and fix what we can.
